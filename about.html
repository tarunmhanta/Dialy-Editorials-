Bridging Public Policy & Management Learning

Transforming daily national news editorials into structured, exam-oriented management studies for MBA first-year students.

🎯 Objective & Purpose

MBA first-year curricula require students to quickly connect theoretical concepts—such as monetary policy, fiscal deficit, market structure, supply chain resilience, and corporate strategy—with real-world business events.

MBA Editorial Daily automates this bridge. Every morning, our system analyzes key opinion pieces and editorials from The Indian Express, extracts management relevance, simplifies complex jargon, and highlights actionable takeaways for classroom discussions, case competitions, and exams.

⚡ How the Platform Works

  1. Automated Discovery: Python routines monitor the latest published editorials from The Indian Express.
  2. Clean Extraction: Page noise, ads, and navigation links are stripped to isolate core editorial arguments.
  3. Gemini AI Educational Synthesis: Google Gemini AI processes the text using specialized MBA prompts to extract management implications, subjects, and key takeaways.
  4. Static GitHub Database: Results are validated into schema-compliant JSON files stored directly within our public repository and published statically to GitHub Pages.
